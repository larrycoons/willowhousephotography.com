FAQ

Answers to the questions clients usually ask first.

This page is built as a starting point and can be expanded as the business learns the questions people ask most often.

What should I wear?

Soft neutrals, muted earth tones, and textured fabrics will fit the Willow House look well. The goal is simple and timeless rather than trendy or overly styled.

Where do sessions take place?

Studio sessions are in Lubbock, Texas. Outdoor sessions are offered in Hobbs, Lubbock, and surrounding areas.

How does booking work?

A simple inquiry is the first step. After that, the session type, date, and next steps can be confirmed in a warm, straightforward process.

What happens after booking?

Clients can expect clear communication, session preparation guidance, and a gallery delivery experience that feels easy and personal.
