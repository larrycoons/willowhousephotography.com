About

The story behind Willow House Photography.

Willow House Photography began with a love for old family photographs and the feeling they carry. The goal is simple: help people feel cared for and beautifully seen while creating images that can live in a family for generations.

Why I started

Because photographs can hold memory.

This brand was built around the quiet, meaningful moments that often become the ones people treasure most later on. A newborn wrapped in a blanket. A child’s laugh. A season of growth. A portrait that one day becomes part of a family’s history.

Every session is meant to feel calm, personal, and genuine instead of rushed or overly staged.

Brand values

  • Warm, welcoming experience
  • Faith-informed service, open to everyone
  • Natural imagery with a timeless feel
  • Comfort, confidence, and care in every session
