Outdoor Sessions

Natural light portraits with room to breathe.

Outdoor sessions are ideal when you want a warm, relaxed look with West Texas sky, open space, and a little movement in the frame.

Locations

Hobbs, Lubbock, and surrounding areas.

Outdoor sessions can take place in fields, open spaces, parks, or other locations that match the feel of the session. Seasonal mini sessions will be offered through the year as the business grows.

Best for

  • Portraits
  • Couples and engagements
  • Maternity
  • Family
  • Children’s milestones and birthdays
  • Graduation and branding

Travel

Travel outside the normal area may be available.

Travel beyond Hobbs, Lubbock, and the surrounding areas may be available for an additional fee depending on the session type and location.
